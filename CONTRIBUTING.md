# Contributing to StillWorld

Thanks for your interest in StillWorld. The project is at an early stage, so
please open an issue to discuss significant changes before investing in them.

## Clean-room provenance

StillWorld is a clean-room project. By contributing, you confirm that your
contribution is your own original work, or that you have the right to submit it
under the project's license.

Do not submit code, schemas, templates, prose, assets, or implementation details
derived from:

- the old Era of Darkness repository;
- leaked source code, including leaked Kings of Chaos source; or
- any other material you are not licensed to use.

Other games may inspire broad, genre-level ideas only. If you are unsure whether
something is acceptable, ask in an issue before submitting it.

## Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO)

All commits must be signed off to certify the
[Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/):

```sh
git commit -s -m "Describe your change"
```

This adds a `Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>` line to the commit
message. Pull requests with unsigned commits may not be merged.
